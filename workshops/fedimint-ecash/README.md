# Fedimint & eCash Workshop

> Community banking without the bank. Private payments without the surveillance.

---

## Overview

This workshop series covers federated eCash systems — Fedimint and Cashu — that enable communities to hold funds collectively with complete payment privacy. Participants learn to set up federations, send eCash globally, and understand how blind signatures create untraceable transactions.

**Format**: Online + In-person
**Frequency**: Monthly
**Location**: Discord + Bitcoin House Bali
**Partners**: Fedi (Community Master of SEA), Fedimint
**Audience**: Intermediate Bitcoiners
**Cost**: Free

---

## What We Cover

### Fedimint Fundamentals
- What is a federated mint and why it matters
- How guardians manage a federation
- Trust model: federated vs custodial vs self-custody
- Setting up a Fedimint federation on Umbrel

### Fedi App
- Private messaging within a federation
- Shared custody model
- Mini apps on Fedimint federations
- Community management features

### Cashu
- Blind signatures and bearer tokens
- Nutstash wallet setup
- Sending and receiving eCash
- Mint discovery and trust

### Privacy Chain
- On-chain → Lightning → eCash (complete privacy)
- Submarine swaps in practice
- Cross-border payments without a bank
- Successfully sent eCash to US, India, Lithuania, Australia, and China

## Run This Workshop in Your City

1. Set up a Fedimint federation on Umbrel (takes ~30 minutes)
2. Pre-fund the federation with a small amount of sats
3. Have participants install the Fedi app
4. Walk through: join federation → receive eCash → send to each other → discuss privacy
5. Budget 1.5-2 hours
6. Reach out to Fedi for community support: they have Community Masters in each region
