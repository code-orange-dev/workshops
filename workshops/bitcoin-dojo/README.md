# Bitcoin Dojo Study Cohort

> 7 weeks of building Bitcoin from scratch. Keys, curves, transactions, Script.

---

## Overview

Bitcoin Dojo is a 7-week study cohort. Based on Jimmy Song's "Programming Bitcoin," participants implement Bitcoin's cryptographic primitives from scratch in Python and graduate into open-source contribution.

**Format**: Online (Discord) - weekly calls, Monday 11:00 UTC
**Duration**: 7 weeks
**Based on**: Jimmy Song's *Programming Bitcoin*
**Audience**: Developers with basic Python knowledge
**Cost**: Free

---

## After the cohort

Paths participants can take next:
- **Chaincode Labs BOSS Challenge** - structured open-source contribution program
- **Good First Issues** across Bitcoin Core, rust-bitcoin, and BDK
- **Grant applications** to OpenSats, Btrust, Brink, Maelstrom, Spiral, HRF
- **Advanced study** - rawBit cohort, Bitshala incubator

---

## Cross-Pollination

- Reading Club participants feeding into Dojo cohorts
- Dojo graduates mentoring in rawBit study cohort
