# Sovereign Bitcoiner Workshop

> Learn to run a node, secure your own keys, mine bitcoin, and protect your privacy.

---

## Overview

The Sovereign Bitcoiner Workshop is Code Orange's flagship in-person workshop series. It covers the four pillars of Bitcoin sovereignty: full nodes, self-custody, mining, and privacy. Designed as a hands-on Socratic seminar where attendees do most of the talking.

**Format**: In-person, 2-3 hours
**Frequency**: Monthly
**Locations**: Bitcoin House Bali, Bitcoin Learning Centre (Chiang Mai)
**Audience**: Beginners to intermediate Bitcoiners
**Cost**: Free

---

## What We Cover

### Pillar 1: Full Nodes
- Why run your own node (don't trust, verify)
- Setting up Umbrel or Bitcoin Core
- Connecting your wallet to your own node
- Tor for privacy

### Pillar 2: Self-Custody
- Single-sig with SeedSigner (air-gapped signing)
- Passphrase protection with Trezor
- Multisig with Nunchuk (2-of-3)
- Defending against a $5 wrench attack: opsec, decoy wallets, multisig
- Seed phrase backup best practices

### Pillar 3: Mining
- How mining works (proof of work, difficulty, nonce)
- Hands-on hashing with SoloSatoshi BitAxe
- Solo mining vs pool mining economics
- Pleb mining philosophy

### Pillar 4: Privacy
- Why privacy matters for Bitcoin
- Lightning payment tracking difficulty (3+ hops)
- eCash with Fedi and Cashu
- Submarine swaps (on-chain → Lightning → eCash)

## Run This Workshop in Your City

1. Download the slides: [GitHub](https://github.com/code-orange-dev/sovereign-bitcoiner-slides)
2. You'll need: a laptop with Umbrel/Bitcoin Core, 1-2 hardware wallets, a BitAxe (optional)
3. Follow the Socratic method - ask questions, let attendees discover answers
4. Budget 2-3 hours, keep groups under 15 for hands-on quality
