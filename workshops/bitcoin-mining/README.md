# Bitcoin Mining Workshop

> Hash with a real miner. Understand proof of work from the inside.

---

## Overview

The Bitcoin Mining Workshop gives attendees a hands-on experience of what Bitcoin mining actually does. Using a SoloSatoshi BitAxe ASIC miner, participants see real hashing in action, understand difficulty and nonce, and learn the economics of pleb mining.

**Format**: In-person
**Frequency**: Quarterly
**Location**: Bitcoin House Bali
**Audience**: All levels
**Cost**: Free

---

## What We Cover

- How proof of work secures Bitcoin
- SHA-256 hashing and the nonce
- Difficulty target and adjustment
- Block rewards and halvings (currently 3.125 BTC)
- Solo mining vs pool mining economics
- The philosophy of pleb mining and decentralization
- Hands-on: watch the BitAxe hash live
