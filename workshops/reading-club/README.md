# Bitcoin Reading Club

> Read, discuss, and earn eCash.

---

## Overview

The Bitcoin Reading Club is a monthly online session where community members read and discuss foundational Bitcoin and cypherpunk texts. Hosted by Alex Xie (@realalexxie), the sessions include quizzes where winners receive Fedi eCash prizes. The Reading Club has evolved to include privacy experiments and hands-on eCash distribution.

**Format**: Online (Discord)
**Frequency**: Monthly
**Host**: Alex Xie (@realalexxie)
**Audience**: All levels
**Cost**: Free

## Format

1. Read the assigned text before the session
2. Open discussion - key takeaways, questions, debates
3. Quiz on the reading material
4. Winners receive Fedi eCash
5. Privacy experiments - sending eCash across countries
