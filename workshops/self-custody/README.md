# Self-Custody Workshop

> Your keys, your coins. Learn to defend your bitcoin against every threat model.

---

## Overview

The Self-Custody Workshop is the most frequently run workshop at Code Orange. It covers everything from basic single-sig setups to advanced multisig configurations that protect against physical attacks. The signature moment: showing attendees how to defend against a $5 wrench attack.

**Format**: In-person
**Frequency**: Monthly
**Location**: Bitcoin House Bali
**Audience**: All levels
**Cost**: Free

---

## What We Cover

### Beginner
- Why self-custody matters
- Seed phrases - generation, backup, storage
- Single-sig wallet setup with SeedSigner (air-gapped)
- Seed phrase backup sheets (distributed to every attendee)

### Intermediate
- Passphrase protection with Trezor ("25th word")
- Understanding derivation paths
- Connecting hardware wallets to your own node

### Advanced
- Multisig with Nunchuk (2-of-3)
- OP_CHECKMULTISIG - how it works under the hood
- Defending against a $5 wrench attack:
  - Good opsec (don't advertise your stack)
  - Decoy wallets (small balance visible, real funds hidden)
  - Multisig (attacker needs multiple devices/locations)
- Frostsnap for collaborative key management

## Run This Workshop in Your City

1. You need at least 1 hardware wallet (SeedSigner is cheapest DIY option)
2. Print seed phrase backup sheets: [template](https://github.com/code-orange-dev/seed-phrase-backup-sheet)
3. For multisig demo, you need 2-3 signing devices + Nunchuk or Sparrow
4. Budget 2-3 hours
5. Socratic method works best - ask questions, let participants discover
